## One-liner
**Emoji Mirror 3D** reads your face and hands through your webcam and shows the matching emoji as a big 3D emoji, live in your browser.

## Short description (for a link preview or post)
Make a face and get the emoji back as a giant 3D emoji that turns with your head. It recognises more than 90 face and hand emojis, from 😂 and 😜 to 🤫, 👍 and 🫶. Play a 10-round challenge, send friends a link to beat your score, and unlock the whole collection. It runs entirely in your browser, and your camera feed never leaves your device.

## Full description (for a README or landing page)

**What it is**
Emoji Mirror 3D is a single-file web app that turns your webcam into an emoji mirror. It tracks your face and hands, works out which emoji you're making, and shows it as a large, thick 3D emoji that follows your head movements.

**What you can do**
- **Mirror:** more than 80 face emojis are detected from your expression, including smiles, winks, tongue-out, kisses, surprise, sadness, anger, sleepy and more.
- **Hand signs and gestures:** 👍 ✌️ 🤟 👌 ✊ ✋ 👋 🙏 🫶 and others, plus hand-on-face gestures like 🤭, 🤫, 🙈 and 🤔. Shaking your head gives 🫨, and chattering your teeth gives 🥶.
- **Group mode:** up to four faces at once, each with its own emoji.
- **Emoji Challenge:** a 10-round game with a countdown, timer, streak bonuses, sound effects and a results screen. It also has a head-to-head mode for up to four players.
- **Collection:** unlock each emoji by making it. Tap any emoji to see it in 3D and get a hint.
- **Make it yours:** a one-time **Tune** routine fits the app to your face, and you can teach it your own version of any emoji.
- **Practice mode:** a live meter shows how close you are to an emoji and what to change.
- **Share:** save a photo of your emoji, make score and collection cards, or send a challenge link so friends can play your exact 10 emojis and try to beat you.

**Privacy**
All tracking happens on your device. No video or images are uploaded or stored. Only your unlocked emojis, high score, tuning and taught faces are saved, in your own browser.

**How to use it**
1. Open the page in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari and click **Start camera**.
2. Make a face, or show a hand.
3. Press **▶ Play** for the challenge, or open **Collection** to see what's left to unlock.

**Tech notes**
It uses MediaPipe for face and hand tracking, three.js for the 3D emoji, and Microsoft's open-source Fluent 3D emoji artwork. It's one HTML file with no build step. It needs an internet connection to load these libraries, and it works best with good lighting and your face turned toward the camera.

**Good to know**
Expression detection is a guess from facial movement, not a read of how you feel, and it can be wrong, especially in poor light or with glasses. Challenge links only work for friends if the page is hosted online.
